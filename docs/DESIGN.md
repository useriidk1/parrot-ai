\# FRONTIER



\## The Game



\*\*FRONTIER\*\* is a persistent multiplayer game built around \*\*rotating 

limited-time campaigns\*\*. Each campaign is a temporary, self-contained 

war or event with its own factions, rules, objectives, and rewards. 

When a campaign ends, its outcome becomes permanent history, and the 

next campaign begins — potentially with a completely different 

setting, genre, or mechanic.



Players build a permanent identity across campaigns: titles, badges, 

cosmetics, and records earned in past events remain on their account 

forever.



\## Pitch



\*\*FRONTIER is a persistent multiplayer game where players join 

AI-led factions across rotating limited-time campaigns, fighting 

to shape each war's outcome and permanently recording their place 

in the game's history.\*\*



\## Campaign Structure



\### Layer 1 — Permanent (the game)

\- Player account and identity

\- Earned badges, titles, cosmetics

\- Lifetime stats and records

\- Historical archive of every past campaign



\### Layer 2 — Temporary (the current campaign)

\- Active factions

\- Live objectives, actions, and scoreboards

\- A countdown timer

\- A winner, declared when the timer ends

\- Exclusive rewards distributed to participants



When a campaign ends:

\- Layer 2 rotates to a new campaign

\- Layer 1 keeps everything the player earned
